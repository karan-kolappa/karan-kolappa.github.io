Privacy Policy

KolKomicz Uploader · Last updated November 8, 2026

What this app is

KolKomicz Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the KolKomicz YouTube channel (@kolkomicz) to post the channel's own videos on a schedule. It is not offered to the public.

Google and YouTube data the app uses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel;
  • set each video's title, description, tags, privacy setting and thumbnail;
  • read the list of the channel's own uploads, so the same video is never posted twice.

The app does not access any other Google data, and it does not collect any information about viewers.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the service that runs the uploads, described below.

Storage

The Google sign-in is handled through Composio (composio.dev), an integration service that keeps the access token securely and sends the upload requests on the owner's behalf. Videos and their text wait in the owner's Google Drive until they are posted. The app keeps no other copies.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Removing it stops all uploads immediately.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: kolkomicz@gmail.com.
