KolKomicz Uploader

The posting tool behind KolKomicz

KolKomicz recaps Marvel comic books in short videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, so people who can't get the comics can still follow the stories.

KolKomicz Uploader posts the channel's own finished videos to the KolKomicz YouTube channel on a schedule. For each video it sets the title, description, tags and cover image that the channel's owner prepared.

It is a private tool. Only the channel's owner uses it, to manage their own channel. It is not offered to anyone else.
